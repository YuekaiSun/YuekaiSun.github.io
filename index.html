Current researchJune 2026 – present

Causal Benchmark Stress Tests

Separating Propensity Overlap and Placement

Training · Supervisor: Prof. Jin-Hong Du

We study how the placement of treatment probabilities across covariates changes statistical information and estimator performance, even when the propensity-score distribution stays the same.

Our framework separates overlap from placement while keeping outcome laws and treatment effects fixed. We develop a Mean–Residual Generator to evaluate whether fitted benchmarks reproduce the source’s response to assignment changes.

Earlier research2026

Multi-treatment Effect Estimation

ACIC 2026 Data Challenge

Supervisor: Prof. Jin-Hong Du · University of Hong Kong

Manuscript (PDF)

I built a pipeline for estimating effects across five treatment arms, combining plasmode-based estimator selection, cross-fitted causal learners, model averaging, and bootstrap inference.

Our follow-up study examines covariate support, outcome distributions, and adjustment for average effects, and explains why accurate outcome prediction does not necessarily yield accurate treatment-effect prediction.
